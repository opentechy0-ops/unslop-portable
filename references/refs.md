# References

Finished screens by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)). Look at them before the Visuals pass. Study their composition, material, lighting and restraint, but do not copy their subject, palette, layout or visual identity. Use them as quality references while creating a distinct visual direction for the current product.

What they share: one bright element per screen, quiet chrome around it, a visual that can be explained in one sentence, and headlines placed on space that was left empty for them.
